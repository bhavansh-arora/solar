Why it matters

Electricity is a big slice of a home's footprint

The EPA's national average grid emissions factor works out to about 0.39 kg of CO₂ for every kilowatt-hour delivered to a home. With the average US household using 865 kWh a month (EIA, 2024), that's roughly 4.1 tonnes of CO₂ a year, just from electricity.

Solar panels produce electricity with no emissions while they run. Every kilowatt-hour they make is one the grid doesn't have to generate.

Where our numbers come from

We use public data from government agencies and established research organisations. Calculator figures are estimates, not a quote; actual savings and impact vary by location, roof, usage, utility rates and local grid mix.

  1. U.S. Energy Information Administration — average residential electricity price 16.5¢/kWh (up from 16.0¢ in 2023) and 865 kWh/month per customer in 2024. eia.gov
  2. U.S. EPA, Greenhouse Gas Equivalencies Calculator — 3.94×10⁻⁴ t CO₂/kWh (eGRID national average, incl. line losses), 4.29 t CO₂e per passenger vehicle per year, 0.060 t CO₂ per urban tree seedling per year (10-year growth), 8.887×10⁻³ t CO₂ per gallon of gasoline. epa.gov
  3. IRENA, Renewable Power Generation Costs in 2023 — global weighted-average LCOE of utility-scale solar PV fell from $0.460/kWh (2010) to $0.044/kWh (2023). irena.org
  4. Solar Energy Industries Association — the US surpassed 5 million solar installations in 2024. seia.org
  5. Zillow Research — homes with solar sold for 4.1% more on average (2019). zillow.com
  6. NREL, Jordan & Kurtz, "Photovoltaic Degradation Rates — An Analytical Review" — median degradation of 0.5%/year across ~2,000 field measurements. nrel.gov

Calculator method

Annual production = (monthly bill × 12 ÷ $0.165/kWh) × 95% offset. Production declines 0.5% per year. CO₂ avoided = lifetime kWh × 0.000394 t. Equivalents use the EPA factors above. Savings assume a 2.5% yearly utility price increase, ~1,400 kWh per kW per year and ~$2,800 per kW installed — these are typical assumptions, and your advisor replaces them with figures for your home.
