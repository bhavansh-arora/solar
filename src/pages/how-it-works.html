Going solar, step by step.

We do the heavy lifting — design, permits, installation and utility approval. Here's exactly what happens and what you'll need to do (not much).

  1. 1

    Free consultation & design

    Tell us about your home and share a recent electric bill. We map your roof, check shading and orientation, and design a system sized to your real usage.

    We do

    Roof analysis, system design, savings & impact report, financing options.

    You do

    Answer a few questions and share a recent bill.

  2. 2

    Site survey & final design

    A technician visits to confirm roof condition, electrical panel capacity and measurements, so your final design and price are exact.

    We do

    On-site inspection, final engineering, written proposal.

    You do

    Be available for a short visit and review the proposal.

  3. 3

    Permits & approvals

    We prepare and file building permits and your utility interconnection application. Timing depends on your city and utility.

    We do

    All permit paperwork, HOA documents if needed, utility application.

    You do

    Sign the forms we prepare.

  4. 4

    Installation day

    Our certified crew installs racking, panels, inverters and any battery or charger. Most home systems are installed in a single day.

    We do

    Install, wire, clean up, and walk you through the system.

    You do

    Give us access to the roof and electrical panel.

  5. 5

    Inspection & switch-on

    Your city inspects the work and your utility grants permission to operate. Then we switch your system on and connect your monitoring app.

    We do

    Schedule inspections, coordinate with the utility, activate monitoring.

    You do

    Download the app and start watching your savings.

After switch-on

We don't disappear after install day.

  • Live monitoring of production, savings and CO₂ avoided
  • Alerts if your system underperforms
  • 25-year warranty on panels, inverters and workmanship
  • Help adding a battery or EV charger later
