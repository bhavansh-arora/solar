FAQ

Questions? Straight answers.

How much does solar really cost?

It depends on your roof, usage and local prices. Many homeowners choose $0-down financing with a monthly payment close to or below their current bill; cash buyers pay more upfront but see the largest lifetime savings. Your advisor shows every option side by side.

Will solar work on my roof?

Most roofs facing south, east or west work well. We assess shading, pitch and roof condition before you commit to anything — and we'll tell you honestly if it isn't a fit.

What happens when the power goes out?

With a battery, your home switches to backup power automatically. Without one, grid-tied systems shut off during outages for line-worker safety.

Are there incentives or rebates?

Many states, cities and utilities offer rebates, performance payments or net-metering credits. Programs change often, so your advisor checks what's available at your address when building your plan.

How long do panels last?

Well over 25 years. NREL's review of field data found a median output loss of about 0.5% per year, so a well-installed system keeps producing the large majority of its original power for decades.

Does solar increase my home's value?

A 2019 Zillow analysis found homes with solar sold for 4.1% more on average than comparable homes without it. Results vary by market.

How do you calculate the environmental impact?

We estimate the electricity your system produces and multiply it by the EPA's national average grid emissions factor. Equivalents (cars, seedlings, gasoline) use the EPA Greenhouse Gas Equivalencies Calculator. Your actual impact depends on your local grid mix.
