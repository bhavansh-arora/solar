FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

How much does solar really cost?

Most homeowners go solar with $0 down and a monthly payment lower than their current electric bill. Cash buyers pay more upfront but see the biggest lifetime savings. Your advisor will show every option side by side.

Will solar work on my roof?

Most roofs facing south, east or west work great. We assess shading, pitch and roof condition with satellite imaging before you commit to anything — and we'll tell you honestly if it isn't a fit.

What happens when the power goes out?

With a Sunvera battery, your home switches to backup power automatically in milliseconds. Without a battery, grid-tied systems shut off during outages for line-worker safety.

Are there incentives or rebates?

Many states, cities and utilities offer rebates, performance payments or net-metering credits. Programs change often, so your advisor checks every incentive available at your address when building your plan.

How long do panels last?

Our Tier-1 panels are warrantied to still produce at least 87% of their original output after 25 years, and they typically keep working well beyond that.

Does solar increase my home's value?

Studies have consistently found that homes with owned solar systems tend to sell at a premium compared with similar homes without solar.
