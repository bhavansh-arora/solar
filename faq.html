Cost & financing

How much does solar really cost?

It depends on your roof, usage and local prices. Many homeowners choose $0-down financing with a monthly payment close to or below their current bill; cash buyers pay more upfront but see the largest lifetime savings. Compare financing options.

How long until solar pays for itself?

It varies with your electricity rates, sunshine, system cost and any incentives. Our calculator gives a quick estimate, and your advisor calculates an exact payback period for your home.

Are there incentives or rebates?

Many states, cities and utilities offer rebates, performance payments or net-metering credits. Programs change often, so your advisor checks what's available at your address when building your plan.

Does solar increase my home's value?

A 2019 Zillow analysis found homes with solar sold for 4.1% more on average than comparable homes without it. Results vary by market, and owned systems are generally viewed more favourably than leased ones.

Installation

Will solar work on my roof?

Most roofs facing south, east or west work well. We assess shading, pitch and roof condition before you commit — and we'll tell you honestly if it isn't a fit.

How long does installation take?

The installation itself usually takes one day. The full process — design, permits, inspection and utility approval — depends on your city and utility. See how it works.

Will installing panels damage my roof?

We use flashed, sealed mounts designed to keep water out, and inspect your roof first. If your roof needs repair or replacement soon, we'll tell you before installing.

Do I need permission from my HOA?

Some HOAs require a review. We prepare the documents you need and help you through it.

Performance

How long do panels last?

Well over 25 years. NREL's review of field data found a median output loss of about 0.5% per year, so a well-installed system keeps producing most of its original power for decades.

Does solar work on cloudy days or in winter?

Yes, at reduced output. Panels produce from daylight, not heat, and we size your system on a full year of production, including cloudier months.

What maintenance do panels need?

Very little. Rain usually keeps them clean, and your monitoring app alerts you if production drops unexpectedly.

Batteries & outages

What happens when the power goes out?

With a battery, your home switches to backup power automatically. Without one, grid-tied systems shut off during outages for line-worker safety.

Do I need a battery?

Not to save money with solar. A battery adds outage backup and can increase savings on time-of-use rate plans. Your advisor will show both options.

Can I add a battery or EV charger later?

Yes. We design systems so storage and EV charging can be added later.

Impact

How do you calculate the environmental impact?

We estimate your system's lifetime electricity production and multiply it by the EPA's national average grid emissions factor. Equivalents (cars, seedlings, gasoline) use the EPA Greenhouse Gas Equivalencies Calculator. See our sources and method.

Is solar really better for the environment?

Solar panels produce electricity with no emissions while operating. Each kilowatt-hour they generate is one that doesn't need to come from the grid's mix of sources.
