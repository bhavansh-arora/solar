01 · Solar panels

Clean power, made at home.

We size your system from a year of your actual usage, then lay out panels around your roof's pitch, orientation and shading to produce as much of your power as possible.

  • Tier-1 all-black panelsHigh-efficiency monocrystalline modules with a clean, low-profile look.
  • Panel-level electronicsMicro-inverters or optimisers, so shade on one panel doesn't drag down the rest.
  • Roof-safe mountingFlashed, sealed attachments, and a roof inspection before we start.
  • Live monitoringProduction, savings and CO₂ avoided, panel by panel, in the app.

02 · Home batteries

Keep the lights on when the grid goes down.

A home battery stores the solar power you don't use during the day, so you can use it in the evening, during peak-price hours, or through an outage.

  • Automatic backupSwitches over on its own when the grid fails — no generator to start.
  • Peak-rate shiftingOn time-of-use plans, run on stored power when grid prices are highest.
  • Choose what's backed upEssentials only, or the whole home with expanded capacity.
Get a battery quote
03 · EV charging

Drive on sunshine.

A Level 2 charger adds range far faster than a standard outlet. Pair it with solar and your daily driving can be powered by your own roof instead of the pump.

  • Level 2 home chargerInstalled, permitted and wired by our electricians.
  • Sized for your carWe add your EV's usage when we size your solar system.
  • Smart schedulingCharge when the sun is shining or when rates are lowest.
